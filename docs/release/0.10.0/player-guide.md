# Getting started with Food Trucks

Food Trucks adds a street-food business to Big Ambitions: start with a cart, expand to a truck and recruit crews for a fleet. This is version 0.10.0, a pre-release for beta and balance testing. Use a new save or back up your existing save before enabling it.

## Install and keep a backup

Subscribe to the Workshop item, let Steam finish downloading, enable Food Trucks in the game's Mods menu and restart. If you had a development build in ModsLocal, remove that duplicate with the game closed and use the Workshop copy before loading your modded save. No other gameplay mod is required; Bakery adds optional products.

Keep a separate copy of your save from before enabling the mod. The local save folders are under:

- Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Hovgaard Games\Big Ambitions\`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/com.Hovgaard-Games.Big-Ambitions/`

Copy the relevant save and any matching companion files to another folder while the game is closed. Keep that backup separate from the saves you continue playing. Steam Cloud synchronisation is not a separate pre-mod backup.

## Open your first cart

1. Complete **Food Safety** at the business school, through the Market Demands door, then call the **City of New York** for the vendor licence.
2. Buy a cart in person at **General US Trucks**. Call the City again for the cart's vending permit.
3. Buy a **Hot Dog Grill** and hot dogs for a simple first menu. Carry the equipment to the cart and load it, then load the food. The Industrial Grill is a different machine: it serves burgers and kabobs.
4. Push to a legal pavement spot, let go, click the cart and choose **Open for business**. If opening is refused, read the reason. Use **Work the window** when you want to resume after stopping.
5. Watch your stock and queue. Use the native work fast-forward or work-duration slider if you want to advance service. Stopping work pauses your own service; a crew can continue if it is available.

Sales count in the day's books as they happen, but both player and crew takings are paid into your balance at **midnight**. Keep money aside for food, supplies and your living costs during the day.

## Match food to equipment

| Equipment | Food and drink |
| --- | --- |
| Hot Dog Grill | Hot dogs |
| Industrial Grill | Burgers and kabobs |
| Industrial Fryer | Fries |
| Pizza Oven | Pizza |
| Industrial Coffee Machine | Coffee and tea |
| Bakery Showcase | Croissants, donuts and cupcakes |
| Ice Cream Counter | Ice cream |
| Slushie Machine | Slushies |
| Concessions Display Case | Popcorn and cotton candy |
| Drinks Fridge or Large Drinks Fridge | Soda |
| Dumpling Steamer | Dumplings |
| Griddle | Tacos and chopped cheese |
| Corn Grill | Grilled corn |

A cart has two equipment slots; a truck has four. Each piece holds its own food, with more room per piece on a truck. Foods served by the same piece share its capacity. **Extra Storage** uses a slot and doubles the other pieces' food capacity. The display on the vehicle changes with the equipment you fit.

The new foods and machines come from commissary orders. With Bakery installed, compatible coffee and display equipment also supports latte, americano, bread, buns and chocolate cookies. You stock and sell the finished products; there is no ingredient recipe system to configure.

## Use a commissary

Visit a marked commissary bay to discover its contact. Call **Spaces and standing orders → Spaces** to rent, move or give up a vehicle's space. Then use **Order stock** for next-morning supplies, or arrange daily/weekly standing orders.

Phone deliveries are paid on arrival. The $250 delivery fee is shared by orders delivered at the same commissary that morning. Food phone orders have a weekly limit across the fleet; as you grow, warehouse logistics can supply commissaries too.

In a bay, open the vehicle's **Commissary stock** controls. **Fill up** loads food, **Take** chooses food or equipment, and **Return** gives it back. Taking equipment fits it; returning equipment also returns food that no longer fits. Supplies already held at a commissary can be transferred in its bay even if that particular vehicle rents elsewhere.

Clean at the commissary where your vehicle rents its space. Returning to that bay overnight allows the overnight cleaning check and avoids a commissary overnight fine.

## Upgrade and hire

Buy a truck at the dealer and obtain its permit. Drive to a legal parking spot, step out and open for business; you work at the serving hatch. Use **Back to the hatch** to return after stepping away.

For a crewed business, complete **Basic HR**, recruit for the **Street food fleet** through a recruitment agency, and assign available employees on the vehicle's BizMan page. A cart has one crew position; a truck has two.

Plan stops for each weekday on **Week plan**, and use **Schedule** to set employee shifts. Allow time for travel and the return to the bay. Crews travel off screen and can restock at their rented commissary bay. Check phone to-dos and BizMan alerts for missing staff, stock or route problems.

## Remove before unsubscribing

For every affected save, keep the mod enabled, load the save, let go of the cart and step out of the truck. Press **Ctrl+Shift+F11 twice within 10 seconds**. The console alternative is `foodtrucks_uninstall` twice.

Wait for the successful removal/refund message, then save. If cleanup reports an incomplete removal or refund, keep the mod installed and retry. Once all affected saves are cleaned, close the game and unsubscribe. Simply unsubscribing cannot perform cleanup; historical transaction/message labels can show raw keys after removal.
