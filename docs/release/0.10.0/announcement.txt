HART. Street Food is ready for community beta and balance testing in Big Ambitions.

Start with a cart, choose your equipment and menu, work the window yourself and grow into trucks and staffed routes. There are 19 food and drink options without another mod, including dumplings, tacos, chopped cheese and grilled corn, plus commissaries, catering and fleet management.

This is a pre-release. Please use a new save or back up your existing save before trying it, and read the removal instructions before unsubscribing.

I would especially like feedback on starting costs, the jump from cart to truck, crew profitability, restocking and the foods or locations you enjoy using. Bugs and confusing steps are useful too. Please post in the Workshop item's Discussions and include your version and settings.
