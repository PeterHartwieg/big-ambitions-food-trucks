# Food Trucks 0.10.0 pre-release

Initial Workshop pre-release for community beta and balance testing.

- Food carts and driveable food trucks, with visible fitted equipment and customer queues.
- Twenty food and drink options, including new dumplings, tacos, chopped cheese and grilled corn; five additional products with optional Bakery integration.
- Player service with fast-forward, timed work and live stock display.
- Crew recruitment, native employee scheduling and vehicle routes planned per weekday.
- Commissary spaces, chosen stock transfers, standing orders and warehouse logistics.
- Location and time-based demand, weather and seasons, premium/event pitches, catering, regular customers, health grades and venue partnerships.
- Street food pages in BizMan, phone to-dos, midnight sales payouts and financial/tax integration.

We recommend using a new save or backing up an existing save. Prices, profitability and progression are being tested and may change. Use the Workshop discussions for bugs and balance feedback, and follow the uninstall instructions before unsubscribing.
