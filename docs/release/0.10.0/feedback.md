## Balance and progression

How does street-food trading fit into your game? Ordinary play is useful: there is no need to find the perfect route or optimise every price. A few complete days are more helpful than one busy hour. Record earnings and costs for the same days; midnight payment timing means money in the bank during the day differs from sales already earned.

```text
Food Trucks version:
Game difficulty and economy settings/mods:
Other enabled mods:
In-game days covered:
Vehicle(s), equipment and menu:
Product prices (default or custom):
Locations and opening hours:
Player-operated, crew-operated or mixed:
Crew count and approximate skill:
Weather/season if relevant:

Sales and stock cost over these days:
Wages, rent, delivery fees and other operating costs:
One-off vehicle/equipment/licence purchases (list separately):
Restocking method and trips/deliveries per day:
How often did you sell out or wait without customers?
What felt too easy, too slow, too profitable or not worthwhile?
What was confusing or tedious?
```

Pick whichever stage interests you:

- **First business:** start with a cart using ordinary funds and prices. Could you afford equipment, supplies and a commissary space? Was it clear what to do? How much did daily living and restocking interrupt service?
- **Truck and crew:** move to a truck, try serving yourself and try staffing a route. Did the upgrade justify its costs? Did wages, travel and restocking leave a worthwhile return?
- **Fleet and supply:** run several vehicles, use standing orders or warehouse supply, and try different districts or menus. Does growth create interesting choices? Does one setup crowd out the alternatives?

Use a new save or a backup for testing. Balance changes are expected during the pre-release.
