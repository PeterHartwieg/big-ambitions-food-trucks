Version 0.10.10
- Commissaries now show their address. The commissary's phone contact shows the building's address and a View on map button, and the Commissary space in BizMan shows the address with a Set destination button (it leads to the building; the loading bay is beside it). Welcome, rental and call texts name the street too. This works for spaces rented in older saves as well.
- Safe uninstall now also finds trucks and carts you left with the private driver. They are removed and refunded like the rest of your fleet, and the mod only says it is safe to remove once none are left.
- Pasting a day (or pasting to all days) on a vehicle's Schedule tab no longer copies the source day's opening hours. Each day keeps its route's hours.
- Quitting the game while serving at a cart or truck window no longer logs a stock-panel error.
