# Street Food 0.10.7

Cart workers now stand behind the cart at the cash register. Employees and the player used to stand off to one side, away from the register. They now stand straight across the counter from it, facing the customers, whichever way the cart faces and on either side of the queue. This also holds after you move the cart or load a save. The register moves to the side the queue forms on, and the first customer waits level with it. Employees also use a safe pavement fallback when their usual place would be on the road or in a parking lane. The register and drinks fridge follow a new queue side immediately, and the first customer stays aligned after navigation placement. Food trucks are unchanged.

Includes the 0.10.6 update: a dedicated drinks-fridge place on carts and trucks, increased soda capacity and demand, and custom truck branding.
